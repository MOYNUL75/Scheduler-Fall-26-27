# Code of Conduct

Schedula welcomes academic, technical, and research discussion conducted with professionalism.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas, evidence, and implementation details rather than attacking people.
- Keep feedback specific, actionable, and relevant to the project.
- Respect the project's unofficial status and intellectual-property restrictions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Misrepresenting the project as an official AIUB system.
- Pressuring the maintainer to open-source, transfer, or permit reuse of the work.
- Posting private, sensitive, or unauthorized institutional data.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, close, or delete comments and issues that violate these expectations.
Repeated or severe violations may result in being blocked from participating in the repository.
